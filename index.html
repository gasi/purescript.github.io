The concrete heart

Make illegal states unrepresentable.

Stop passing String and Number around. Your domain isn’t made of primitives — it’s made of UserId, Email, SKU, PasswordHash. Encode each constraint in a type once, and the compiler enforces it everywhere.

Transfer.purs
Four interchangeable arguments. Swap two and it still compiles.
NonEmpty String

A string that is guaranteed non-empty — the empty case can’t reach your code.

Money

Won’t let you add dollars to euros. The currency travels inside the value.

UUID

Validated once at the boundary, then trusted everywhere after — never re-checked.

Constraints belong in types, not in documentation that goes stale. This is soundness you can see.
